Acme keeps your customers, your scheduled work and your private notes in one place for teams working across several sites, with a complete record of who changed what.

Sign in

No sign-up, no card, no e-mail address. You get your own copy of Acme with a week of sample work already in it. It is deleted automatically after 24 hours, so please do not put real customer data in it.

What it does

  • Work across sites

    Each site keeps its own book. Staff are attached to the sites they work at, and see the customers and jobs of those sites only.

  • A diary that refuses double bookings

    Nobody is booked twice at ten o'clock and no customer is expected in two places at once. The same rules run as you type and again on the server.

  • Customers, with their history

    Contact details, every job they have had and what is still open — on one screen rather than four.

  • Private notes stay private

    Notes belong to the people who wrote them. The front desk can book and reschedule and never reads a word of them.

  • A full audit trail

    One entry per changed field: who, when, from what, to what. The trail obeys the same access rules as the records it describes.

  • Roles that mean something

    Owner, front desk, staff and a customer portal. What each one can see is enforced by the server, not by hiding buttons.
